3. Game design (what we are building)

Pillars

PillarMeans in play
Stealth over shootingAvoid 2 patrols + checkpoint; getting seen is the fail state.
Survival pressure72-hour clock; Act 2–3 add hunger/cold bite.
Choices with costCheckpoints: papers timed dialogue, bribe, or sneak.
Mood over polysLow-poly URP; audio lo-fi .wav 22kHz mono.

Acts and acceptance

Act 1 (vertical slice): 1 street 60x20m, win by pure stealth in <5 min, lose + retry <3s. Act 2: Papers or River. Act 3: DMZ minefield + flare, carry choice. Controls: WASD move, Shift sprint, C crouch, E interact, Q whistle, F flashlight, Esc pause. Detail: docs/GAME_DESIGN.md.
