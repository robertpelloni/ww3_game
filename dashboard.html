🖇 4. Game Systems & Survival Architecture
CORE MECHANICS

Stealth mathematics and survival formulas verified in StealthSim and implemented in Act 1 Unity C#.

Dynamic Light Exposure Model Tested

ConditionExposure FactorGameplay Impact
Curfew Spotlight 1.00 (100%) Instant suspicion surge & chase trigger
Flashlight On 0.85 (85%) High visibility to nearby patrols
Street Lamp Radius 0.50 (50%) Moderate visibility, suspicious if running
Alley Shadow / Darkness 0.05 (5%) Concealed; guards must be within 1.5m to see
Formula: EffectiveVisibility = StanceVisibilityMultiplier × ExposureFactor. In shadows, crouching visibility drops to 0.6 × 0.05 = 0.03.
